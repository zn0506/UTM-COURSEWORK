# ULRS1032
INTEGRITY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION COURSE

This course covers the basic concept of corruptionincluding the value of integrity, anti-corruption, formcorruption, misuse of power in activitiesdaily and organization and methods of preventioncorruption. 
Cases related to corruption are also discussed. 
Teaching and learning methods implemented in the form of 'learning' based on experience' through individual activities and groups. 
At the end of this course, students can understand integrity practices, the concept of corruption, anti-corruption and abuse of power as well prevention of corruption in society and organization.
